<div class="research-kicker">Computational magnetism · neutron scattering · scientific software</div>

<p class="hero-lede">I connect models of complex magnetization textures to the neutron-scattering signatures that can actually be measured.</p>

I am a postdoctoral researcher and computational scientist at the [Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter](https://www.mpsd.mpg.de/) in Hamburg, where I work on the EU-funded [MaMMoS project](https://mammos-project.github.io/). My work combines analytical theory, numerical simulation, and scientific software development across atomistic spin dynamics and micromagnetics.

<div class="thesis-statement">
My research asks what can be reliably inferred about complex magnetization structures from neutron-scattering data—and under which assumptions. The goal is not only to reproduce an observable, but to understand which parts of a model the observable can truly identify.
</div>

## Research focus

<div class="focus-grid">
  <section class="focus-card">
    <div class="eyebrow">01 · Inference</div>
    <h3>Magnetic neutron scattering</h3>
    <p>Connecting polarized and unpolarized SANS observables to nonuniform spin structures, angular anisotropies, and experimentally distinguishable model features.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="focus-card">
    <div class="eyebrow">02 · Modelling</div>
    <h3>Reduced magnetic descriptions</h3>
    <p>Developing Green-function, perturbative, polynomial, and vortex representations that remain physically interpretable while reducing simulation complexity.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="focus-card">
    <div class="eyebrow">03 · Software</div>
    <h3>Reproducible computation</h3>
    <p>Building GPU-accelerated and analysis-focused tools, including NuMagSANS and MuMag2022, and contributing to reproducible multiscale workflows in MaMMoS.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## From assumptions to observables

<div class="research-flow" aria-label="Research workflow">
  <div class="flow-step"><strong>Physical assumptions</strong><span>Interactions, geometry, disorder, symmetry, and boundary conditions.</span></div>
  <div class="flow-step"><strong>Model & simulation</strong><span>Analytical theory, atomistic spins, micromagnetics, and reduced descriptors.</span></div>
  <div class="flow-step"><strong>Scattering signature</strong><span>Predictions that can be compared with measured SANS observables.</span></div>
</div>

## Experimental context

Alongside my computational work, I have participated in neutron-scattering beamtimes at the [Institut Laue–Langevin](https://www.ill.eu/) and the [ISIS Neutron and Muon Source](https://www.isis.stfc.ac.uk/). My role has focused on theory, data reduction, MuMag2022-based analysis, micromagnetic modelling, and physical interpretation. Research stays at the University of Oxford and at Lund University/LINXS have supported these connections between modelling, software, and experiment.
